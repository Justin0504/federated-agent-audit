\caption{The federated, center-blind pipeline. \textbf{(1)} Inside each agent the
raw \textcolor{figrose}{content} is \textcolor{figindigo}{labeled} and
\textcolor{figemerald}{hashed} in process. \textbf{(2)} What leaves is a
desensitized edge---a one-way hash plus \ext{} labels, zero content
bytes---and it is the only thing that crosses the trust boundary. \textbf{(3)} The center runs four
detectors over the resulting metadata graph and \textbf{(4)} emits violations. It
never sees content, and provably cannot recover it (Lemma~\ref{lem:blind}).}
\label{fig:arch}
