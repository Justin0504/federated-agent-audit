01 / task design — proposed

120 workflow clusters

Scheduling, travel, creator collaboration and support. Balance group sizes 4, 8 and 16 across clusters.

02 / paired conditions — proposed

Same task, different views

Three membership conditions × four reporting views = 1,440 proposed episodes per model/seed. Not independent tasks; not executed.

03 / separate outcomes

Two adversaries, one ledger

Recipient inference and auditor re-identification; task success, audit coverage, abstention and cost. No assumed winner.

Display rules are scripted. No privacy score is computed. Pseudonymization is not a differential-privacy mechanism. Narayanan & Shmatikov, De-anonymizing Social Networks (2009) supplies background on topology-based linkage, not results for this demo.
